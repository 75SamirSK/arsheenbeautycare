Live Orders

Where your real orders show up

1. Orders tab (live): every order placed on any customer's phone or computer syncs here in real time via a cloud database — no matter which device it was placed on. Update order status here and customers can track it.

2. Email (Formspree): every order is also emailed to the Gmail you connect in Settings → Formspree Endpoint, as a backup.

3. WhatsApp: customers also get a "Message us on WhatsApp" button with the full order pre-filled.

If the cloud connection is ever unavailable, the Orders tab automatically falls back to showing a local backup copy saved on this device only.
